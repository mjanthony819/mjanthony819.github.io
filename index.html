SkinLens

medical imaging · live demo

Skin lesion analysis app. Upload a dermoscopy photo and a classical image-processing pipeline (hair removal, Otsu segmentation, morphology, ABCD features) returns the segmented lesion and an explainable risk score. No deep learning. It refuses to score bad images.

Python·OpenCV·Streamlit

live app ↗source ↗
the fine printSegmentation checked on 250 held-out ISIC images: mean Dice 0.66, against 0.53 for plain Otsu thresholding. The risk score is a screening aid, not a diagnosis, and has not been clinically validated.

Does ML Beat the Market?

quant finance · backtest

An end-to-end backtest on 6 NSE stocks plus Nifty. Momentum, volatility and volume features feed gradient boosting, retrained every year on an expanding window (walk-forward) and paper-traded out of sample with 0.1% costs.

Python·yfinance·Gradient Boosting·Walk-forward

source ↗
the fine printResult: no statistically detectable edge. Main 6-stock walk-forward: -1.25% CAGR, excess Sharpe -0.54 (CI -1.573 to 0.462, which spans zero). Equal-weight: +0.54% CAGR, -0.36. Nifty BeES: +7.35% CAGR, +0.13. Direction accuracy 48.98% against a 51.02% majority-class baseline. Published as a rigor exercise, not a discovery.

No-code backtesting for Indian stocks. Pick a Nifty stock, a strategy and a date range, and it replays the trades day by day with costs - equity curve against buy-and-hold and Nifty, drawdown, Sharpe, and a trade list you can export.

the fine printSix rule-based strategies (SMA crossover, RSI mean reversion, momentum, Bollinger, Donchian breakout, buy-and-hold). Educational tool, not investment advice.

Classical Image Restoration Lab

image processing · live demo·Python, OpenCV, FastAPI, React

Seven classical restoration and enhancement methods - gamma, CLAHE, histogram equalization, Dark Channel Prior dehazing, Retinex - with parameter controls, intermediate stages, histograms and PSNR/SSIM scoring. Built as an investigation tool: why an image improves, which method wins, and what gets lost.

Digital image processing course project. The hosted demo sleeps when idle, so the first visit can be slow to load.
