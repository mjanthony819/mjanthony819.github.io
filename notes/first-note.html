A place to
think out loud

15 OCT 2026   /   SAMPLE

This is a replaceable layout sample, not a personal blog post by Anthony. The words below show the format; the next words can be yours.

Some things don't need to be a project. A thought after class. A problem that took longer than expected. Something worth coming back to.

A note is enough. A title, a date, and a little space to follow the thought.

Start with one thing

This is where the actual story goes. What caught your attention? What changed your mind? What are you still trying to understand?

It can be a short entry or a long one. It can have a conclusion, or simply stop at a question.

Leave room

Not every entry needs an answer. Sometimes writing it down is the useful part.

To make this yours: copy this file, change the title and date, and replace the paragraphs. Add its title and link to the notes index. A short guide is in notes/README.md in the repository.
